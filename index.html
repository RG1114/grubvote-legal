GrubVote Privacy Policy

Also see our Terms of Service

Effective date: 22 September 2026

1. Summary

GrubVote collects the minimum data needed to run a group vote: who you are in a room, what you voted for, and the meeting area you choose. We do not sell your data. Rooms and their data are temporary by design.

2. Data we collect

• Account data: if you sign in with Google, your name, email address, and profile photo URL. As a guest you simply choose a nickname.
• Room data: your nickname, a device identifier used to recognise you in your own rooms, your votes (dietary choice, cuisines, vibe, dining style, budget), and the join time.
• Location you provide: the area you pick or search for on the map when joining a room. GrubVote never tracks your live GPS route, and your picked area is stored only inside the room you join.
• Approximate device location: only where you grant the OS permission, and only to show distances to restaurants and to display the map around you; it is not used as your room location unless you pick it.
• Preference data: taste preferences you optionally save on your device to speed up future votes, stored locally on your phone.
• Advertising and diagnostics: AdMob may serve personalised ads under Google's privacy policy; crash and usage information may be collected to fix bugs.

3. How we use data

To compute a fair restaurant ranking for your group, to let members see who joined and who has voted, to display distances and maps, to personalise suggestions when you opt in, and to keep the service running and free. We do not build behavioural profiles, sell data, or share it for third-party marketing.

4. Where data is stored and for how long

Room data (participants, votes, results) is hosted on Supabase servers in the India region for the life of the room and a short period afterwards. Locations you pick travel inside the room's participant records and disappear with them. Locally saved preferences live on your own device and can be cleared any time from the app or by uninstalling. We aim to keep stored data only as long as needed to operate the service.

5. Who we share data with

Only service providers that help us run the App: Google (sign-in, advertising), Supabase (hosting/database), and our map and place data providers. We may also disclose data if legally required (for example, by a valid court order) or to protect the safety of users or the service.

6. Your choices

You can decline Google sign-in and stay anonymous as a guest, skip picking a location (the group can still vote using the host's area), turn off personalised ads at the OS level (Ad Settings), and clear your saved preferences any time. Signing out clears your profile from the device.

7. Deletion

You can delete your account and data at any time with the "Delete Account & Data" button on your profile screen: it removes the rooms you hosted and the votes you cast from our server and erases everything saved on this device. You can also ask the room host to end or leave the room, clear app storage in your phone settings, or contact us and we will remove what we can identify, within a reasonable time.

8. Children

GrubVote is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security and changes

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and access is limited to what the App itself needs. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
