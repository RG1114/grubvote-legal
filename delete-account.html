How to delete your account and data

GrubVote lets you delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app. No email or support ticket is required.

  1. Open the GrubVote app and sign in (or open it as a guest).
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the “Delete Account & Data” button.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deletion is completed immediately after confirmation.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes the following data permanently:

  • Your GrubVote profile (name, email, and photo if you signed in with Google; nickname if you used guest mode)
  • All voting rooms you hosted, including their participant lists, votes, and results
  • All votes and locations you submitted in rooms hosted by others
  • Your saved taste preferences and all data stored locally on your device
  • Your authenticated session on all devices

What may be retained

Anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage analytics collected by Google Firebase may be retained without any identifier that can be linked back to you. Nothing that identifies you personally is kept after deletion.
